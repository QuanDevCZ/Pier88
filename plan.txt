PIER88 COAST - HOMEPAGE STUDY REBUILD
=====================================

Goal
----
Recreate the homepage of https://pier88coast.com/ as a local study piece: same layout,
section order, type scale, colours, scroll choreography (GSAP + Lenis) and pacing.

Boundaries (agreed scope)
-------------------------
- All HTML/CSS/JS is written from scratch. The original site's source code is NOT copied.
- The restaurant's photos, videos and paid fonts (Tan Mon Cheri, Golden Hopes, Stevie Sans)
  are NOT downloaded or reused. Substitutes:
    Tan Mon Cheri  -> Italiana (Google Fonts, thin high-contrast caps)
    Golden Hopes   -> Allison (Google Fonts, thin script)
    Stevie Sans    -> Jost (Google Fonts, geometric sans)
  Photos: free Unsplash-licence photos, hotlinked (non-Plus only).
  Illustrative pieces (plates, sea waves, sketch icons) are drawn in CSS/SVG.
- Copy is rewritten in the same spirit, not lifted verbatim.
- Local only. Never deploy publicly - it imitates a real business's site.

Stack
-----
Zero build. index.html + styles.css + main.js. GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger and Lenis 1.3.x
from cdnjs/jsdelivr. Serve with `npx serve .`.

Known differences vs original
-----------------------------
- Fonts are free look-alikes, so letter shapes differ (esp. the script).
- Photos are different subjects; hero shrimp-on-fork cut-out is a round photo on a CSS plate.
- No background videos (original uses video in atmosphere / waves); stills with scroll motion instead.
- Lottie animations on the original are not reproduced.
- Burger menu and popup are visual only.
